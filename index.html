Engineered for Commuters

Smart ETA Tracking

Our Time-Based mode adapts to live traffic and transit delays. We continuously recalculate your arrival time so you wake up precisely on schedule.

Distance-Based Alarms

Prefer predictable alerts regardless of traffic? Switch to Distance Mode to trigger your alarm exactly 2 kilometers before reaching your destination.

Anti-Theft Protection

Sleep securely in public. Turn on Anti-Theft mode to trigger a loud, continuous alarm if someone attempts to move or snatch your phone while you rest.

Smart Audio Routing

Wearing earphones? The alarm routes directly through them so you don't disturb other passengers. No earphones? It plays loud and clear on the speaker.

Unstoppable Alerts

Built to bypass modern Android Doze restrictions. Our alarm punches right through your lock screen and silence settings to guarantee you wake up.

Battery Optimized

By intelligently syncing GPS polling with your current travel speed and distance, we minimize battery drain so you have juice left when you arrive.
